Course reference:
- argmax returns an argument achieving the maximum, not the maximum value. A=UΣVᵀ implies Avᵢ=σᵢuᵢ. For orthonormal W, projection is XWWᵀ and squared reconstruction error is ||X||²_F−||XW||²_F. PCA picks the top singular directions to maximize retained variance. Center data first.
- KNN assumes nearby inputs have similar conditional outcome probabilities. Scaling affects distance. Larger k generally reduces variance but increases bias. Split data before fitting preprocessing; tune on validation data and evaluate the whole pipeline on held-out test data.
- Linear regression predicts wᵀx+b and fits squared error. Logistic regression applies sigmoid to a linear score and fits binary cross-entropy. Classify at probability 0.5 when false-positive and false-negative costs are equal. A convex J satisfies J(αu+(1−α)v) ≤ αJ(u)+(1−α)J(v) for 0≤α≤1. Gradient descent updates w←w−η∇J(w). Regularization penalizes complexity; training and test error need not agree.
- Backpropagation uses the chain rule, multiplying local derivatives. ReLU(z)=max(0,z), with derivative 1 for z>0 and 0 for z<0. For L=½(s−y)², dL/ds=s−y. In training, clear gradients, compute the forward pass and loss, backpropagate, and update parameters.
- Convolution shares learned filters across spatial locations. A stride-1 stack of n kernels of width k has receptive-field width 1+n(k−1), absent dilation. Padding affects output size, not the amount of real input available at an edge. ResNet uses skip connections. U-Net combines downsampling with upsampling and skip connections for dense segmentation. Compare architectures using a consistent evaluation protocol.
- Autoregressive models factor sequence probability as ∏ₜ p(xₜ|x₍<ₜ₎). A causal attention query at position i masks keys j>i, not earlier keys or itself. Attention applies softmax to scaled query-key scores and takes a weighted sum of values. Position information distinguishes order. Top-k sampling renormalizes the probabilities of the k retained tokens. Greedy decoding picks the largest probability.
- An autoencoder learns an encoder and decoder to reconstruct inputs. A VAE learns a distribution over latents, balancing reconstruction against KL divergence from a prior; samples can be decoded from the prior. With x̃=x+σϵ and standard normal ϵ, predict either x or ϵ: x̂=x̃−σϵ̂. Noise prediction at multiple noise levels enables generation from noise toward data.
- For agent-assisted development, break work into verifiable modules and check behavior with tests. Visible output or confidence alone does not establish correctness.
